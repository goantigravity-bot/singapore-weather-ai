The Challenge

What Problem Are We Solving?

⚠️ The Gap

NEA's current weather services provide broad, city-wide predictions (e.g., "Thunderstorms in the afternoon") that lack the spatial and temporal granularity needed for the public to make confident, real-time decisions about outdoor commutes and activities in Singapore's hyper-local tropical climate.

📍 Not Localised

City-wide forecasts don't tell you if your street will get rain

⏱️ Not Timely

Updates every few hours — weather changes in minutes in the tropics

🎯 Not Actionable

No route-aware or activity-specific guidance for daily decisions

Result: The public experiences a persistent gap between the forecast they receive and the weather they actually encounter — leading to disrupted plans, safety concerns, and diminished trust in public weather services.

Methodology

KPI Design Principles

All 30 KPIs across 6 impact areas follow four guiding principles to ensure they are meaningful and actionable.

1

Quantifiable

Every KPI has a clear numerical target — not vague "improvements." This enables objective tracking and accountability.

2

Measurable

Relies on system logs, API data, or existing government surveys — no additional measurement infrastructure needed.

3

Incremental

Targets are based on industry benchmarks (MSS, GovTech CSAT) or realistic improvements over current baselines — not aspirational moonshots.

4

Attributable

Each metric can be traced back to Weather AI's direct contribution, not confounded by external factors or unrelated initiatives.

30 KPIs across 6 Impact Areas — each with a clear target, measurement method, and rationale grounded in industry benchmarks and Singapore government standards.

Our Approach

How Does This Solution Address the Problem?

🛰️ + 📡 → 🧠 Data Fusion

Fusing Himawari-9 satellite imagery with NEA's real-time sensor network through a CNN + LSTM deep learning model.

📍 Hyper-Local Predictions

Street-level rainfall forecasts powered by IDW interpolation across sensor stations — not just regional averages.

⚡ Real-Time (<200ms)

Predictions updated with the latest 10-minute sensor readings for true real-time intelligence.

🛣️ Route-Aware Intelligence

Weather along a commuter's entire path, sampled every 2km — not just start/end points.

🔄 Self-Improving Accuracy

Automated daily retraining ensures continuous adaptation to seasonal patterns.

In essence: Transforming NEA's existing meteorological infrastructure into an AI-powered public intelligence layer — turning raw data into actionable insights that save time, reduce risk, and enable better daily decisions.
